Vinícius Júnior

Vinícius Júnior had an amazing 2023/2024 season with Real Madrid

Vinícius Júnior won La Liga and the UEFA Champions League. Individually, he won the UEFA Champions League Player of the Season award and was named The Best FIFA Men’s Player.

He finished the season with 24 goals and 11 assists in all competitions.
